PETAL WAKE — RECORDED ORCHESTRAL SOUND LIBRARY

32 excerpts from 32 different orchestral movements/works. Each excerpt is 90 seconds and comes from a real instrumental performance, not MIDI or a synthesized orchestra.

Musopen commissioned and released these performances for reuse. Musopen identifies the recordings as public-domain material in its project announcement; the linked Internet Archive compilation is marked Public Domain Mark 1.0.
Publisher statement: https://musopen.org/blog/musopen-kickstarter-project-1-complete/
Compilation: https://archive.org/details/musopen-dvd
Rights metadata: https://archive.org/metadata/musopen-dvd
Public Domain Mark: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/

Edits: excerpts selected, loudness normalized to -20 LUFS / -3 dBTP target, gentle boundary fades, encoded as stereo AAC at 192 kb/s and 44.1 kHz. No pitch or tempo alteration.
