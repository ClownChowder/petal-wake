Upcoming shifts

Use a live iCalendar subscription link. Saved links and downloaded calendar data are encrypted on this phone. For Limited view, use a work-only calendar: every busy block may become an alarm. Anyone who obtains a sharing link may be able to read its shared information.

Rouse tests the replacement first. Once it works, delete the old link in your calendar service. Proton may take up to eight hours to publish changes.

Ready for your first work alarm

No sharing link?

Export your work calendar as an .ics file, then import it here.

Imports are snapshots. Import again when your schedule changes.

Allow time for schedule changes. Rouse requests updates roughly every 15 minutes; Android and your calendar service can delay refresh. Check the last download and your next alarm after a shift changes.

Sounds

Choose a gentler start to your day.

Softer orchestra and solo piano passages, with gentler peaks. All included offline.

Add your own music

Choose a genre, then pick audio from your phone. Imported music is copied into Rouse for dependable offline alarms. Linked music stays in its original location and may stop working if that file or its permission changes.

Choose up to 40 files. Imports: up to 100 MB each and 500 MB total. Preview a file before relying on it for an alarm. Personal music stays on this phone and is not included in a settings export.

Find music privately
  1. Open the official Tor Browser yourself and visit commons.wikimedia.org. Look for a gentle recording, not a MIDI file.
  2. On that file’s page, check the recording’s license and any credit requirements, then download the audio. You do not need to sign in.
  3. Return here, tap Import music files, choose the download, and preview it before setting an alarm.

Rouse does not contact the music site when importing a local file. Tor Browser protects only the browsing done inside it.

Orchestra collection

28 additional recordings are available as an optional offline download.

The smaller app includes gentle music, nature sounds, standard tones, and four orchestra recordings. The pack is about 60 MB, hosted on GitHub, and requires a one-time download agreement. Once saved, it plays offline.

Lock-screen privacy

Your alarm still rings and opens normally. Android may show the generic Rouse alarm notice before you unlock.

Private calendar links

Links and downloaded calendar files are encrypted on this device. Copying a saved link requires your phone’s security check.

Move Rouse to another phone

Save one password-protected package with your calendar sharing links, rules, personal alarms, themes, alarm history, and personal music. Linked music becomes an offline copy in the package.

Keep the file and its password separately. Anyone with both can read your calendar links. The package is encrypted independently of this phone, so it can be opened on a new phone. Rouse cannot recover a forgotten password.

Moving to a new phone

  1. On this phone, save a full move package and copy it to storage you trust.
  2. Install Rouse on the new phone, then restore the package into a new or empty setup.
  3. Allow Android alarm and notification permissions. Work alarms remain paused until you enable them.
  4. Check the next wake time and phone alarm volume, then run a test alarm.
  5. After the new phone works, remove Rouse from the old phone. Keep the shared calendar link active on the new phone. To rotate it, connect a new link first, then revoke the old one.

Android permissions and existing system alarms cannot be transferred. Only future personal alarms are restored. Unavailable linked music must be reconnected before saving the package.

Alarm preferences

Work schedule

Leave blank for a work-only calendar or Limited view link. Title matching requires a Full view link with event titles.

All-day calendar blocks have no start time. No alarm will ring for them until you enable this and enter the actual shift start time. Your wake-up lead time is then applied.

Shifts between these times use the calendar’s regular lead time. All-day shifts also use the regular lead time.

Snooze & playback

Optional. The backup uses a standard ring at the selected phone alarm volume without a gradual rise.

Choose a longer limit if you need more time to wake. A finite limit protects the phone’s battery if an alarm is left unattended.

Maximum-strength pulses with short pauses.

Changes not saved. Save preferences before leaving this page.

Keep Rouse ready

Check that Android allows Rouse to run in the background. If you use Do Not Disturb, allow alarms in that mode.

Alarms are restored after restart once the phone is unlocked. Android force-stop disables alarms until you open Rouse again. Alarms ring for the duration you choose above.

About Rouse

Rouse means to awaken or stir into activity. This Four Wags app helps you set personal and work-shift alarms, with optional calendar-linked wake times.

Made with care by Four Wags. Thank you to the open-source developers and everyone who helped test and improve it.
